Important Information

  • New Jersey's statutory fee for acknowledgments, oaths, affidavits, and proofs of deed is $2.50 per act.
  • Business service and travel fees are separate from the statutory notarial fee.
  • The signer must personally appear before the notary as required by New Jersey law.
  • Peak Notary Services does not prepare documents or provide legal advice.
  • Final pricing depends on the number of signers, documents, notarial acts, and appointment location.
