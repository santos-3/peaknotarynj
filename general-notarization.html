MOBILE SERVICE

Peak Notary Services can travel to your home, office, hospital, facility, or another agreed location.

Mobile appointments starting at $25

Important Information

  • The New Jersey statutory notarial fee for the listed general acts is $2.50 per act.
  • Mobile travel fees are separate from the notarial fee.
  • The signer must personally appear before the notary as required by New Jersey law.
  • Valid identification may be required.
  • Peak Notary Services does not provide legal advice or prepare legal documents.
