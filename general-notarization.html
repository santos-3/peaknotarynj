WHAT'S INCLUDED

Your appointment includes travel to the agreed location, identity verification, completion of the authorized notarial act, and proper completion of the notarial certificate and journal entry as required.

The applicable New Jersey notarial fee is charged separately from the mobile travel/service fee.

Before Your Appointment

  • The signer must personally appear before the notary.
  • Have the document ready before the appointment.
  • Do not sign the document in advance unless instructed otherwise by the notary.
  • Bring acceptable identification when required.
  • Peak Notary Services does not prepare documents or provide legal advice.
  • Travel/service fees will be disclosed before the appointment is confirmed.
